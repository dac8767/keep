KEEP

Raise a castle. Protect your sovereign. Survive ten sieges.

Found your realm — survey the map and click to place your Keep.

Build phase (5:00) — gather lumber, stone, ore and food; build farms, quarries, mines, a bloomery, houses and medical tents; wall in your King.

Defense phase — armies march in formation from the red arrows at the map edge, with siege trains, rams and cavalry reserves. Subjects shelter in the Keep; you can still raise walls, gates and engines and train soldiers.

Beasts — giant spiders and cave trolls attack anyone near their lairs. Wall one in and it may guard your castle… if it doesn't eat your builders first.

Moats — dig trenches that touch a lake or river and they flood. Connect a lake with a tentacle monster and it may swim in to defend you.

You lose if your King or Queen is slain or captured, all subjects are gone, or every structure falls.
